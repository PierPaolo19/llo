📖 How to Use

  1. Connect Wallet: Click "Connect Wallet" to connect MetaMask or another Web3 wallet
  2. Check Network: Ensure you're on the correct network (Ethereum Mainnet, Sepolia, etc.)
  3. View Liquidity: See available USDT liquidity in the pool
  4. Deploy Receiver: Deploy your flash loan receiver contract implementing IFlashLoanReceiver
  5. Execute Flash Loan: Enter receiver address and amount, then execute
  6. Provide Liquidity: Deposit USDT to earn fees from flash loans
